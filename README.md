# Wear App Store Companion 📱⌚

A premium Material 3 Android smartphone companion application designed to easily manage, install, and update Wear OS watch applications wirelessly over local Wi-Fi.

## Features ✨
*   **AMOLED Premium Dark UI**: Formatted with high-contrast Material 3 cards and clean animations.
*   **Zero-Config Connection**: Automatically remembers your watch's IP and connection ports.
*   **Native Wireless Debugging Pairing**: Includes an expandable pairing settings card allowing you to pair your phone to the watch directly using standard TLS + SPAKE2 pairing codes. No computer or ADB CLI required!
*   **Single-Tap Sideloading**: Tap "Install" on any app in the feed, and the companion app automatically downloads the corresponding APK from the GitHub releases database, streams it to the watch, and triggers installation.
*   **Full Filesystem Explorer**: Navigate directories on the watch's internal storage (`/sdcard`), upload photos/files from the phone, delete old files, and create folders natively.
*   **Over-The-Air Self Updates**: Checks the repository releases feed to self-update the phone app automatically.

## Getting Started 🚀
1.  Enable **Developer Options** and **Wireless Debugging** on your Wear OS watch.
2.  Open the pairing options inside the companion app, enter the **Pairing Port** and **6-Digit Pairing Code** displayed on the watch, and tap **Pair**.
3.  Enter the watch's **Connection Port** at the top and tap **Connect**.
4.  Begin installing apps and managing files!
